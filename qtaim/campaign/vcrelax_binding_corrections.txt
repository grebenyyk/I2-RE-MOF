IMPORTANT — smearing handling
-----------------------------
The loaded (I2-containing) polyiodide is borderline metallic (I-I sigma* at
E_F). At degauss=0.01 Ry (Table 1) the SCF does NOT converge during cell
optimization (Fermi-bisection oscillation), so the loaded vc-relax was run at
degauss=0.03 Ry. The smearing contribution to the energy is ~geometry-
independent, so it CANCELS in the strengthening (loaded_fixed and loaded_vc at
the same 0.03). The empty framework is closed-shell (degauss-insensitive) and
was run at 0.01. Thus the strengthening values below are smearing-consistent.
Absolute fixed-cell values are ~5 kJ/mol I2 less bound than Table 1's 0.01
values (the smearing shift); the strengthening itself is unaffected.

KEY FINDING — deep minimum = benzene-sandwiched I2 + uniform polyiodide chain
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
(Distances below use the CORRECT Cartesian conversion for the non-orthogonal
cell, f0*a1+f1*a2+f2*a3. An earlier version of this table used a transposed
cell and was wrong; this supersedes it.)

The equilibrium (deep) vc-relaxed structure is the SAME motif for all three
metals: ONE discrete I2 molecule at 2.82 A (inversion-centered, sandwiched
between two rotated benzene rings) plus a uniform ~2.94 A polyiodide chain
running centered down the pore. I-I distances (A), fixed-cell (Table 1) ->
deep minimum:

  Gd fixed: I2 2.79, 2.79          + chain 2.91,2.91,3.03,3.03,3.14,3.14
     deep:  I2 2.82                + chain 2.94,2.94,2.95,2.95,2.95,2.95 (uniform)
  Tb fixed: I2 2.79, 2.79          + chain 2.92,2.92,3.02,3.03,3.07,3.08
     deep:  I2 2.82                + chain 2.93,2.94,2.94,2.94,2.94,2.95 (uniform)
  Y  fixed: I2 2.74,2.74,2.89,2.89 + chain 3.01,3.02,3.03,3.04
     deep:  I2 2.82                + chain 2.93,2.94,2.94,2.94,2.94,2.94 (uniform)

So vc-relax converts the fixed-cell arrangement (two I2 dimers + a non-uniform
chain) into ONE benzene-sandwiched I2 + a uniform 3c-4e chain: one of the two
I2 dimers dissociates and joins the chain. This is a host-guest rearrangement
(linker rotation creates the benzene-I2 sandwich wall).

KINETIC TRAPPING (chain-start vs seeded)
----------------------------------------
The chain-start vc-relax strengthenings in the table above (Gd -19.7, Tb -12.2)
are LOWER BOUNDS: starting from the chain geometry, Gd/Tb get trapped in a
shallower minimum (two I2 + partial chain) and do NOT reach the deep minimum.
Y reaches the deep minimum directly (-47.5). Seeding Gd/Tb with Y's deep-minimum
motif lets them reach the same deep minimum (seeddeep runs): loaded energy drop
~ -39 (Gd), -41 (Tb) kJ/mol I2, i.e. comparable to Y's -44.6. So the TRUE
equilibrium strengthening is ~ -40 kJ/mol I2 for all three metals.

Implication for the main text
-----------------------------
The fixed-cell Table-1 geometry (two I2 + chain) is metastable for all three
RE-MOFs; the equilibrium loaded structure is one benzene-sandwiched I2 + a
uniform ~2.94 A polyiodide chain, ~40 kJ/mol I2 lower. This host-guest
rearrangement (rotated linkers, I2 sandwiched between benzene rings, uniform
centered chain) is the result worth showing. The chain-start vc-relax numbers
are path-dependent (kinetic); the seeded/deep-minimum numbers are the
thermodynamic equilibrium.
