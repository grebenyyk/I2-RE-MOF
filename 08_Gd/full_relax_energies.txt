--- CAVEATS ---
* Both runs used conv_thr=1e-3, mixing_beta=0.2, degauss=0.01. The chain SCF
  plateaus at ~7e-4 accuracy at the strained-chain geometry (conv_thr 1e-5/1e-6
  is unreachable there and caused the first attempts to abort at the 300-iter
  SCF cap; the original uniform_chain/loaded.in also accepted ~3e-4 on its first
  SCF). The mol run initially used conv_thr=1e-5 and aborted at step 64 (SCF
  plateau ~1.0e-5); it was restarted from its step-63 geometry with conv_thr=1e-3
  (see attempt1_conv1e-5_64steps/).
* The mol hit nstep=200 (force 0.0103, just above forc_conv_thr=1e-2) -- the mol
  basin is broad/soft; the energy had effectively converged (delta-E < 1e-5 Ry
  over the last ~30 steps, E = -6865.0882 Ry). The chain converged cleanly
  (force 0.0045, 'bfgs converged' at step 63).
* E_int 'vs activated-empty' uses the framework-only-relaxed empty (no I).
  A rigorous full-relax reference needs the empty framework ALSO full-relaxed
  (cell fixed, atoms free). The prior full_relax/empty_full did NOT converge
  (stopped step 48, force 0.011) -- its E is reported for reference only.
* First attempts with mixing_beta=0.1 failed (SCF hit 300-iter cap); the proven
  recipe is mixing_beta=0.2 + degauss=0.01 (matches uniform_chain/loaded.in).
  See full_relax_chain/attempt1_beta0p1/ and attempt2_conv1e-5_stalled/.
