PBE-D3 convergence study on I2@Y-MOFvc (80-atom loaded / 72-atom empty / gas).
All SCFs at conv_thr = 1e-6. ΔEads = (E_loaded - E_empty - 4·E_gas)/4 × 1312.75 Ry→kJ.

=== K-MESH (ecut 50/400; loaded AND empty at mesh k; gas @Γ) ===
  k-mesh    E_loaded (Ry)     E_empty (Ry)     ΔEads (kJ/I2)   Δ vs 4×4×2
  Γ         -5767.508605      -2735.809709     -163.82         -71.11   (Fermi undersampling)
  2×2×1     -5767.228213      -2735.809843      -71.75         +20.96
  4×4×2     -5767.292174      -2735.809950      -92.71          0.00   ← production (loaded)
  6×6×3     -5767.300069      -2735.809951      -95.30          -2.59

  → Loaded polyiodide@MOF needs 4×4×2 (metallic polyiodide bands; Γ off by 71 kJ).
  → Empty MOF: max|Δ vs Γ| = 0.080 kJ/I2; 4×4×2 vs 6×6×3 = 0.0005 kJ ⇒ CONVERGED, Γ adequate.
  → Gas I2 (20 Å box): Δ(2×2×1 − Γ) = 0.000 meV ⇒ Γ exact.

=== CUTOFF (k 4×4×2; empty/gas at matched cutoff) ===
  ecut      ΔEads (kJ/I2)   Δ vs 50/400   step
  40/320      -92.70          +0.09         —
  50/400      -92.79           0.00        -0.09   ← production
  60/480      -92.82          -0.03        -0.03

  Steps shrink 0.09 → 0.03 ⇒ CONVERGING (variational; higher ecut lowers E_loaded
  slightly more than E_empty since loaded has more electrons, so ΔEads grows
  marginally more exothermic by a vanishing amount). Spread 40→60 = 0.12 kJ.

=== CONCLUSION ===
  Production: loaded@4×4×2, empty@Γ, gas@Γ → ΔEads = -92.79 kJ/mol per I2.
  Only the loaded cell needs 4×4×2; empty MOF and gas I2 are converged at Γ
  (empty bias of empty@Γ vs empty@4×4×2 = -0.08 kJ/I2, negligible).
  Ecut 50/400 is within 0.03 kJ of 60/480 and ~0.09 of 40/320 ⇒ converged.
